How Spry holds an app back on this system: . The app in front is never touched. Everything is put back when Spry is paused, switched off or quit.

What Spry cannot do: it does not add memory or make the processor faster. It decides who goes first: while the app in front works, the apps behind it wait and take longer. While it rests, they get the processor back.
